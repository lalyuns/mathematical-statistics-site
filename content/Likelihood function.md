# Likelihood function

The [[Likelihood function]] treats the observed sample as fixed and views the joint density as a function of $\theta$:

$$
L(\theta)=f(x_1,\ldots,x_n\mid\theta).
$$

It is used for [[Maximum likelihood estimator]] and [[Likelihood ratio test]].
