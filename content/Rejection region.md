# Rejection region

The [[Rejection region]] is the set of sample values for which a [[Non-randomized test]] rejects [[Null hypothesis]].

In likelihood ratio testing, this is usually a region where [[Likelihood ratio statistic]] is small or where the likelihood under the alternative dominates the likelihood under the null.

Related: [[Acceptance region]], [[Critical value]], [[LR rejection region sign argument]].
