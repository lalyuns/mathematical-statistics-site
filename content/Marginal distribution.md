# Marginal distribution

A [[Marginal distribution]] is obtained from a [[Joint distribution]] by summing or integrating out unwanted variables.

For continuous $(X,Y)$,

$$
f_X(x)=\int f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dy.
$$

Related: [[Conditional distribution]].
