# Independence

Events $A$ and $B$ are independent if

$$
P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B).
$$

Random variables are independent when their joint distribution factors into marginal distributions.

Independence is stronger than zero covariance. [[Midterm problem - uncorrelated does not imply independent]] is the standard warning.
