# Transformation of random variables

For one-to-one differentiable transformations, densities change by the Jacobian.

If $Y=g(X)$ is monotone,

$$
f_Y(y)=f_X(g^{-1}(y))\left|\frac{d}{dy}g^{-1}(y)\right|.
$$

For multivariate transformations, use the absolute determinant of the inverse Jacobian.

Related: [[Inverse transform sampling]], [[Spherical transformation]].
