# UMP test

A [[UMP test]] maximizes [[Power function]] at every point in the alternative parameter space among a class of tests.

This is stronger than being [[Most powerful test]] at one fixed alternative point.

Related: [[Composite hypothesis]], [[UMP tests are unbiased]], [[Neyman-Pearson lemma]].
