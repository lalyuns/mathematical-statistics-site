# Null and alternative hypotheses

A test compares

$$H_0:\theta\in\Theta_0,\qquad H_A:\theta\in\Theta_A,$$

with $\Theta_0\cap\Theta_A=\varnothing$ and $\Theta_0\cup\Theta_A=\Theta$.

[[Null hypothesis]] is the side controlled by the level. [[Alternative hypothesis]] is the side for which we want high [[Power]].

Related: [[Hypothesis]], [[Hypothesis test]], [[Size and level]].
