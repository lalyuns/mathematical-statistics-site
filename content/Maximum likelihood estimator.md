# Maximum likelihood estimator

A [[Maximum likelihood estimator]] maximizes the [[Likelihood function]]:

$$
\hat\theta=\arg\max_\theta L(\theta).
$$

It often solves log-likelihood score equations, but boundary-dependent models such as uniform distributions may require direct support arguments.

Related: [[Likelihood ratio statistic]], [[Asymptotic normality of MLE]].
