# Asymptotic test

An [[Asymptotic test]] uses a limiting distribution to approximate its null behavior.

[[Wilks theorem]] gives the asymptotic chi-square distribution for likelihood ratio tests.

Related: [[Exact test]], [[Likelihood ratio test]].
