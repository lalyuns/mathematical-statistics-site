# PDF and PMF

A PMF assigns probabilities to discrete values. A PDF integrates to probabilities for continuous values.

For a continuous random variable,

$$
F_X(x)=\int_{-\infty}^x f_X(u)\,du.
$$

Related: [[CDF]], [[Transformation of random variables]].
