# Probability integral transform

If $X$ is continuous with CDF $F$, then

$$
F(X)\sim \operatorname{Uniform}(0,1).
$$

Conversely, if $U\sim\operatorname{Uniform}(0,1)$, then $F^{-1}(U)$ has CDF $F$.
