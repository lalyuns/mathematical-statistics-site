# Test inversion

[[Test inversion]] turns a family of tests into a [[Confidence set]].

If $A(\theta_0)$ is the [[Acceptance region]] of a level $\alpha$ test for $H_0:\theta=\theta_0$, define

$$C(x)=\{\theta_0:x\in A(\theta_0)\}.$$

Then $C(X)$ has coverage at least $1-\alpha$.

Fixed parameter, varying data: test. Fixed data, varying parameter: confidence set.
