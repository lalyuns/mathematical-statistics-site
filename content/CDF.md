# CDF

The [[CDF]] of $X$ is

$$
F_X(x)=P(X\le x).
$$

A CDF is nondecreasing, right-continuous, tends to $0$ at $-\infty$, and tends to $1$ at $+\infty$.

Related: [[PDF and PMF]], [[Probability integral transform]].
