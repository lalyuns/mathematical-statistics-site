# Conditional distribution

A [[Conditional distribution]] divides a joint density by the marginal density:

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_Y(y)}.
$$

Conditioning also supports [[Conditional expectation]] and laws such as [[Law of total variance]].
