# Likelihood ratio statistic

[[Likelihood ratio statistic]] compares the best likelihood under the null with the best likelihood overall:

$$\lambda(x)=\frac{\sup_{\theta\in\Theta_0}L(\theta)}{\sup_{\theta\in\Theta}L(\theta)}.$$

Small values mean the restricted model explains the data poorly relative to the full model.

Related: [[Likelihood ratio test]], [[Wilks theorem]].
