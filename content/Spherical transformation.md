# Spherical transformation

For standard normal $(X,Y,Z)$, write

$$
X=R\sin\phi\cos\theta,\quad
Y=R\sin\phi\sin\theta,\quad
Z=R\cos\phi.
$$

The Jacobian is $r^2\sin\phi$, so the joint density of $(R,\Theta,\Phi)$ is proportional to

$$
e^{-r^2/2}r^2\sin\phi.
$$

This shows the direction is uniform on the sphere and separates from the radius.
