# Joint distribution

A [[Joint distribution]] describes a random vector $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)$.

For continuous variables, its density integrates over regions. For discrete variables, its PMF sums over points.

Related: [[Marginal distribution]], [[Conditional distribution]], [[Independence]].
