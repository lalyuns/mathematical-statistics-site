# Null hypothesis

The null hypothesis is the model claim protected by the testing procedure.

A [[Level alpha test]] controls the rejection probability under the null. A [[p-value]] measures how extreme the observed test statistic is relative to the null distribution.

Related: [[Alternative hypothesis]], [[Type I error]], [[Rejection region]].
