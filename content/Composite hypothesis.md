# Composite hypothesis

A composite hypothesis allows more than one parameter value, such as $H_0:\theta\in\Theta_0$.

Composite hypotheses make optimality harder because [[Power function]] must be compared over a parameter set, not just at one point.

Related: [[Simple hypothesis]], [[UMP test]], [[Likelihood ratio test]].
