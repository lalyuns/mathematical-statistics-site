# Method of moments

[[Method of moments]] estimates parameters by matching population moments to sample moments.

If $\mu_j(\theta)=E_\theta[X^j]$ and $\hat\mu_j=n^{-1}\sum X_i^j$, solve

$$
\hat\mu_j=\mu_j(\theta)
$$

for the unknown parameters.
