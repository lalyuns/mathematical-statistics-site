# Random sample

A [[Random sample]] is a collection of observable random variables $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ that are iid from a common distribution.

It is the default data structure for [[Sampling distribution]], [[Maximum likelihood estimator]], and [[Hypothesis test]] problems.

In finite-population sampling, sampling with replacement gives iid observations. [[Sampling without replacement]] gives observations with the same marginal distribution, but the observations are not independent.

Related: [[Statistic]], [[Sampling without replacement]], [[Sample mean and sample variance]].
