# Conditional expectation

[[Conditional expectation]] $E[Y\mid X]$ is the best mean-square prediction of $Y$ using $X$.

It satisfies the tower property:

$$
E[E[Y\mid X]]=E[Y].
$$

Related: [[Law of total variance]], [[Rao-Blackwell theorem]].
