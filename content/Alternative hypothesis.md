# Alternative hypothesis

The alternative hypothesis is the model claim that a [[Hypothesis testing|test]] tries to detect evidence for.

It is usually written as $H_1$ or $H_a$, and it competes with the [[Null hypothesis]]. The [[Power function]] measures the probability of rejection under values in the alternative.

Related: [[Simple hypothesis]], [[Composite hypothesis]], [[Most powerful test]].
