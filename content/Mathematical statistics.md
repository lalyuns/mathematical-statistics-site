## Core path

1. Start with [[Probability space]], [[CDF]], [[PDF and PMF]], and [[Common distributions]].
2. Move to [[Joint distribution]], [[Conditional distribution]], [[Expectation]], and [[Moment generating function]].
3. Then use [[Random sample]], [[Sampling distribution]], [[Central limit theorem]], and [[Normal sample distributions]].
4. For estimation, follow [[Sufficient statistic]], [[Rao-Blackwell theorem]], [[Lehmann-Scheffe theorem]], and [[Maximum likelihood estimator]].
5. For testing, use [[Hypothesis testing MOC]], [[Neyman-Pearson lemma]], [[Likelihood ratio test]], and [[Wilks theorem]].
