# Sigma algebra

A [[Sigma algebra]] $\mathcal A$ is a collection of subsets of $\Omega$ that contains $\varnothing$, is closed under complements, and is closed under countable unions.

It specifies which [[Event|events]] are measurable.

Related: [[Probability space]], [[Probability function]].
