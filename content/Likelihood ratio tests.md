# Likelihood ratio tests

Likelihood ratio tests are tests built from the comparison between a restricted likelihood under $H_0$ and a larger likelihood under the full model.

The main atomic notes are [[Likelihood ratio statistic]], [[Likelihood ratio test]], [[LR rejection region sign argument]], and [[Wilks theorem]].
