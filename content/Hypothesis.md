# Hypothesis

A hypothesis is a statement about a population parameter, usually written as a restriction on $	heta$.

[[Null and alternative hypotheses]] split the parameter space into competing claims. [[Hypothesis test]] then uses data to decide whether to reject the null side.

Related: [[Simple hypothesis]], [[Composite hypothesis]].
