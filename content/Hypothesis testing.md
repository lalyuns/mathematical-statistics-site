# Hypothesis testing

Hypothesis testing is a decision framework that uses a sample to decide whether to reject a [[Null hypothesis]] in favor of an [[Alternative hypothesis]].

The core objects are a [[Test function]], a [[Rejection region]], a [[Level alpha test]], and a [[Power function]]. Topic 5 develops this framework into [[Neyman-Pearson lemma]], [[Likelihood ratio test]], and [[Wilks theorem]].
