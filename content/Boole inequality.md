# Boole inequality

[[Boole inequality]] says

$$
P\left(\bigcup_{i=1}^n A_i\right)\le \sum_{i=1}^n P(A_i).
$$

It is the union bound. Applying it to complements gives the lower bound

$$
P\left(\bigcap_{i=1}^n A_i\right)\ge \sum_{i=1}^n P(A_i)-(n-1).
$$
