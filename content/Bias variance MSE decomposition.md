# Bias variance MSE decomposition

For estimator $W$ of $\tau(\theta)$,

$$
MSE(W,\tau)=\operatorname{Var}_\theta(W)+Bias(W,\tau)^2.
$$

If $W$ is unbiased, its MSE equals its variance.

Related: [[UMVUE]], [[Bayes estimator]].
