# Moment generating function

The moment generating function of $X$ is

$$
M_X(t)=E[e^{tX}]
$$

when it exists near $0$.

It determines the distribution and turns independent sums into products:

$$
M_{X+Y}(t)=M_X(t)M_Y(t).
$$
