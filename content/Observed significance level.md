# Observed significance level

The [[Observed significance level]] is the p-value of a test.

It is the smallest significance level $\alpha$ at which the observed data would lead to rejection:

$$
p(x)=\inf\{\alpha:x\in R_\alpha\}.
$$

Small p-values mean the observed statistic is extreme under the null in the direction favored by the alternative.

A p-value is not the probability that $H_0$ is true. It is a tail probability computed under $H_0$ or the worst-case null parameter.

Related: [[p-value]], [[Valid p-value]], [[Tail probability p-value]].
