# Non-randomized test

A [[Non-randomized test]] has a [[Test function]] of the form

$$\varphi(x)=1(x\in C).$$

$C$ is the [[Rejection region]]. Its complement is the [[Acceptance region]].

Related: [[Randomized test]], [[Critical value]], [[Likelihood ratio test]].
