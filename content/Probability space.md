# Probability space

A [[Probability space]] is $(\Omega,\mathcal A,P)$.

$\Omega$ is the sample space, $\mathcal A$ is a [[Sigma algebra]], and $P$ is a probability function satisfying Kolmogorov's axioms.

Related: [[Probability function]], [[Event]], [[Bayes rule]].
