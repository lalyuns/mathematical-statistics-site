# Statistic

A [[Statistic]] is any function of the observed sample that does not contain an unknown parameter.

If $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ is a sample, then

$$
S=S(X_1,\ldots,X_n)
$$

is a statistic when it can be computed from the data alone. Examples are $\bar X$, $S^2$, order statistics, and likelihood ratio statistics after unknown parameters have been maximized out.

The probability distribution of a statistic is its [[Sampling distribution]].

Related: [[Random sample]], [[Sample mean and sample variance]], [[Likelihood ratio statistic]].
