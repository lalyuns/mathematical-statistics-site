# Unbiased test

A test is unbiased if

$$\beta(\theta_A)\ge\beta(\theta_0),\qquad \theta_A\in\Theta_A,\ \theta_0\in\Theta_0.$$

The test should reject more often under the alternative than under the null.

Related: [[Power function]], [[UMP tests are unbiased]].
