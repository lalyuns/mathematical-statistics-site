# Common distributions

Topic 1 lists the basic families used throughout the course: Bernoulli, binomial, geometric, negative binomial, hypergeometric, Poisson, uniform, exponential, gamma, normal, Cauchy, beta, and logistic.

These distributions reappear in [[Sampling distribution]], [[Moment generating function]], [[Maximum likelihood estimator]], and [[Bayes estimator]] problems.
