# Convergence almost surely

$X_n$ converges almost surely to $X$ if

$$
P\left(\{\omega:\lim_{n\to\infty}X_n(\omega)=X(\omega)\}\right)=1.
$$

Notation:

$$
X_n\xrightarrow{a.s.}X.
$$

Almost sure convergence is stronger than [[Convergence in probability]]. The strong law of large numbers uses this mode of convergence.

Related: [[Strong law of large numbers]], [[Weak law of large numbers]], [[Convergence in probability]].
