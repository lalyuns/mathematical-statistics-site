# F distribution

If $U\sim\chi^2_m$, $V\sim\chi^2_n$, and $U\perp V$, then

$$
\frac{U/m}{V/n}\sim F_{m,n}.
$$

Ratios of independent normal sample variances produce $F$ distributions.
