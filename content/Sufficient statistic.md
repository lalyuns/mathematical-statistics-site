# Sufficient statistic

A [[Sufficient statistic]] preserves all sample information about $\theta$.

Equivalently, the conditional distribution of the sample given the statistic does not depend on $\theta$.

Equivalently, if $S(X)=s$, then the remaining randomness in $X$ after knowing $s$ is parameter-free.

This is why conditioning an estimator on a sufficient statistic can improve it without changing its target.

Related: [[Factorization theorem]], [[Minimal sufficient statistic]], [[Rao-Blackwell theorem]].
