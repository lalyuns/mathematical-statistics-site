# t distribution

If $Z\sim N(0,1)$, $U\sim\chi^2_k$, and $Z\perp U$, then

$$
T=\frac{Z}{\sqrt{U/k}}\sim t_k.
$$

This explains why $\sqrt n(\bar X-\mu)/S\sim t_{n-1}$ for a normal sample.

Related: [[Studentized sample mean]], [[Chi-square distribution]], [[Normal sample theorem]].
