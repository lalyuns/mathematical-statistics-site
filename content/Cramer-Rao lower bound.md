# Cramer-Rao lower bound

For an unbiased estimator $W$ of $\tau(\theta)$,

$$
\operatorname{Var}_\theta(W)\ge \frac{[\tau'(\theta)]^2}{I(\theta)}.
$$

The bound is governed by Fisher information $I(\theta)$.
